# Circle of Influence

> Everything someone cares about falls inside a larger Circle of Concern, but
> only some of it falls inside the smaller Circle of Influence — what they can
> actually affect. Proactive people spend their energy inside the smaller
> circle; reactive people spend it complaining about the parts of the larger
> one they can't touch, which only shrinks their actual influence further.

## Core Idea

Covey's model nests two circles. The **Circle of Concern** is everything a
person cares about or is affected by — their health, their team's decisions,
the economy, the weather, a manager's mood. The **Circle of Influence**, drawn
inside it, is the subset they can actually do something about. Covey's central
claim is behavioral and somewhat counterintuitive: proactive people focus
their time and energy inside the Circle of Influence — the things they can
act on — and as a result, that circle tends to *grow* over time, because
acting effectively on what's controllable builds credibility, skill, and
relationships that expand what's reachable next. Reactive people focus their
energy on the Circle of Concern outside their influence — blame, complaint,
reactive emotion about things like "the team," "the market," "my manager" —
and that focus produces no leverage, while the energy spent there is energy
not spent growing the smaller circle, so it tends to *shrink* instead.

The distinction isn't about toxic positivity or pretending constraints don't
exist — real external constraints are real. It's about where someone chooses
to spend their limited attention and energy once a constraint is acknowledged:
dwelling on it (Circle of Concern) versus asking what, within it, is still
actually theirs to change (Circle of Influence).

## How Growth OS Applies It

`agents/onboarding-agent.md`'s Phase 3 ("¿Qué está en el camino?") applies this
directly as a redirect, not a dismissal, when a blocker is located entirely
outside the person's control: "Dado que {external blocker} está fuera de tu
control directo — ¿hay algo en cómo reaccionas a eso que sí podrías cambiar?
¿Qué haría diferente alguien que domina moverse en ese contexto?" The move
doesn't deny the external constraint is real — it asks what, inside that
constraint, is still within reach, which is the Circle of Influence's entire
practical content.
