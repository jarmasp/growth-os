# Appreciative Inquiry

> Start by examining what's already working and why, in concrete detail,
> before addressing what isn't — because people and systems tend to grow in
> the direction of what they persistently ask questions about, and a
> deficit-first inquiry tends to produce more of the deficit it's looking for.

## Core Idea

Cooperrider and Srivastva developed Appreciative Inquiry (AI) as a deliberate
alternative to the default "find the problem, fix the problem" approach to
organizational change, which they argued has a hidden cost: constantly
searching for what's broken trains attention, language, and identity around
deficit, and tends to reproduce more of what it's looking for. Their
alternative starts from the opposite end — deliberately and systematically
examining a concrete instance of something that *worked*, in detail, asking
what specifically made it work, who was involved, what conditions were
present — before ever turning to what's deficient.

The method is often summarized as a 4-D cycle: **Discovery** (appreciating
what already works, through concrete stories, not abstract praise),
**Dream** (envisioning what could be, grounded in what Discovery surfaced as
real and possible, not a disconnected fantasy), **Design** (deliberately
building structures and practices toward that vision), and **Destiny/Delivery**
(sustaining the change). The Discovery stage is the one most often
misunderstood as naive positivity — it isn't skipping hard problems, it's a
specific claim about where useful data lives: a close examination of a real
success contains concrete, transferable information (what conditions were
present, what behaviors mattered) that a generic complaint about failure
usually doesn't.

## How Growth OS Applies It

`agents/onboarding-agent.md`'s Phase 5 follow-up for an impact/relatedness-type
motivation answer applies this directly: "¿Puedes recordar un momento donde
sentiste que lo que hiciste llegó a alguien — que tuvo un efecto real? ¿Qué
pasó?" — anchoring the stated motivation in one concrete, positive, already-
lived example rather than an abstract claim about caring about impact. The
agent names this explicitly as "a touchstone for the profile" — a specific
discovered instance that becomes a reference point for calibrating what
"mattering" concretely looks like for this person, following AI's Discovery
stage rather than starting from what's missing.
